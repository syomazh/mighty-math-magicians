## Verdict

**No statement is wrong.** Every audited declaration says what the report's tables claim for it, up to the differences listed there and the ones listed below; every statement agrees with my own numerics; every one depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; I could not derive a false instance. **Three gaps of disclosure**: (1) the Lean proof of the algebraic proof depends, inside Mathlib, on Kummer's theorem and on Legendre's formula, through Mathlib's proof of (x + y)^p = x^p + y^p, which no text says and which the audit of "direct references" cannot see (the main proof, the lattice-path proof and the level-profile theorem do **not** have this dependency, also not inside Mathlib); (2) for arbitrary lists of blocks (C27) the formal statement is an identity between two arithmetic expressions, "minimal" has to be read as "attains the bound", and the number of mixed blocks is computed with a truncated subtraction; (3) the README and one sentence of the report say that the lattice-path formalization differs from the text "in one step", where the report's own table lists two.
