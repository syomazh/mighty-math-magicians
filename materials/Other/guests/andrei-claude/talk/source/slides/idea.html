THE IDEA

Rotate children, don’t permute them

Konvalinka lets the full symmetric group act on the branches at every node: orbit sizes are products of multinomials, and the argument stops at divisibility. We keep only cyclic rotations, a Sylow p-subgroup. Then every orbit has p-power size, and the smallest orbits can be counted mod p.

1

Expand each q-ary node into a block of a p-ary tree

2

Rotation classes have size a power of p

3–4

Digit sums bound the exponent below; equality means a minimal tree

5

Minimal tree: sₚ(m) complete subtrees on a skeleton of t asymmetric nodes; collapse each complete subtree to a leaf labelled by the subtree’s height

6

Consider the same trees but with distinct labels on the leaves

7

Count the labelled trees mod p by collapsing cherries
