A SECOND VIEW

Every node is worth −p

Label all m leaves instead of only the atoms: every node becomes asymmetric, and the same cherry collapse evaluates both sides.

Sandpile picture: p grains on a site topple into one grain on the next. From m grains, a ring of k sites takes N(m) topplings and a half-line takes ν(m). The exponent t is the difference.

This route needs the power of p in m! (proved by the same collapse), so we present it as a remark, not as the proof.

Mighty Math Magicians · Caltech Mathathon 2026

A shorter accounting proof written by our team. With all leaves labelled, each of the N(m) internal nodes contributes a factor p and a sign. The labellings themselves, m factorial, contain nu(m) such factors from the digit forest. The difference is t. In a minimal tree this is visible directly: nu(m) nodes inside atoms plus t skeleton nodes.
