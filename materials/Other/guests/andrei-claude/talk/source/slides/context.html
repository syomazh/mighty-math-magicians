What is known, and what is new

Known

  • Etherington (1939): binary tree shapes have 2ᵈ ordered versions
  • Deutsch–Sagan (2006): the orbit proof with exactness for p = 2, with (2s − 3)!! minimal orbits
  • Kersting–Fischer (2021): the same minimal binary trees, in phylogenetics
  • Petersen, Touchard: count fixed points of a p-cycle, collapse the fixed block

New here, as far as we found

  • The rotation (Sylow) group for every prime p and every q = pᵏ
  • Minimal trees are skeletons on the digits of m, with a depth condition
  • The cherry collapse is a carry mod q − 1: exact valuation and residue, no factorial valuations

Status: every lemma checked by computer on all small cases; the proofs are under line-by-line human review.

Mighty Math Magicians · Caltech Mathathon 2026

For p = 2 the whole orbit argument is Deutsch and Sagan’s, and the class sizes go back to Etherington. The collapse of a fixed block is the classical proof of Touchard’s congruence for Bell numbers. What we have not found in print is the combination for odd p and prime powers: the Sylow group, skeletons with the depth condition, and the cherry collapse as a carry mod q − 1. Our literature search was limited, so we say “as far as we found”.
