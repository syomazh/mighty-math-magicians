STEP 5

Collapse the complete subtrees

A minimal tree is a skeleton of t asymmetric nodes carrying complete subtrees, the atoms. Since leaf counts add without carries, there are exactly mⱼ atoms of height j, where mⱼ is the j-th base-p digit of m: sₚ(m) atoms in all.

Nmin = |S(m, k)|: skeletons with mⱼ leaves labelled j, children taken up to cyclic rotation at every node; a leaf labelled j sits at depth ≡ −j (mod k).

q = 3, n = 8, m = 17 = 122₃: atoms of sizes 9, 3, 3, 1, 1; root adds 3 + 9 + 5 = 010₃ + 100₃ + 012₃ with no carry.

Unwinding the characterization: maximal complete subtrees are atoms, the remaining nodes form a skeleton of t asymmetric nodes. Carry-free addition means digit vectors just add, so the atoms are exactly the digits of m. Conversely any skeleton on the digit atoms is minimal. Atoms are rotation invariant, so minimal classes are cyclic skeletons; in this example there are 10 of them.
