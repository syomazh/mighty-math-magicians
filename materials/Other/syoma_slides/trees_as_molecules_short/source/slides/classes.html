A rotation class is one molecule

IN THE PROOF · ROTATION CLASSES

“Two trees in one class are called rotation equivalent; [T] denotes the class of T.”

A rotation turns the three children of one node cyclically, anywhere in the tree.

C₂⁽³⁾ = 3 plane trees, and they form one class [T].

IN CHEMISTRY · FREE ROTATION

A carbon’s three groups spin freely about the bond toward the root: the same molecule.

Plane trees are drawings; rotation classes are the molecules (mirror forms apart).

Class counts 1, 1, 2, 5, 11, 28, … are the stereoisomer counts of alcohols (OEIS A000625; Blair–Henze 1932), checked to 14 carbons.
