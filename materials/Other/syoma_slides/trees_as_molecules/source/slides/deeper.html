INTERCHANGEABLE HYDROGENS

The spins that fix a molecule sort its hydrogens into at least s₃(2n + 1) groups, and exactly that many when d = t.

Checked by script up to 14 carbons.

AS MUCH SYMMETRY AS POSSIBLE

d = t exactly when the fixing spins form a Sylow 3-subgroup of all permutations of the hydrogens.

Checked by script for n ≤ 6. A restatement of our lemmas.
