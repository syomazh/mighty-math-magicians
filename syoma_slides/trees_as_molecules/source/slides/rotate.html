A rotation is a spin about a bond

IN THE PROOF · DEFINITION OF ∼

“rot_r(U) = (U₁₊ᵣ, U₂₊ᵣ, …, U_{p+r}) … Let ∼ be the smallest equivalence relation such that (R1) (U₁,…,U_p) ∼ rot_r(U₁,…,U_p) for every r; (R2) if Tᵢ ∼ Tᵢ′ for each i, then (T₁,…,T_p) ∼ (T₁′,…,T_p′).”

(R1): rotate the children of one node. (R2): do it anywhere inside the tree.

IN CHEMISTRY · FREE ROTATION

Looking down the bond toward the root, a carbon’s three groups sit 120° apart. At room temperature they spin freely, so all three positions are the same molecule.

Spin 120° each time: the cyclic order (orange, teal, purple) never changes.
