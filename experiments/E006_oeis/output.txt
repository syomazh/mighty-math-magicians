PART 3a -- the odometer as a product of rotations
==============================================================================
Leaf x is addressed by its root path = base-p digits of x, least significant
digit first (d_0 = child index at the root, d_{h-1} = leaf slot). Adding 1
rotates by +1 the deepest node on the path (parent of the leaf, depth h-1)
and then, for every trailing digit equal to p-1, the next node up (carry).
So the nodes at depths h-1, h-2, ..., h-1-j rotate, where j = number of
trailing (p-1) digits of x. The root rotates exactly when x = p^h - 1.
